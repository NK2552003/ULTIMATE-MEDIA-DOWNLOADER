# Code of Conduct

## My Promise

Hey everyone!

I want Ultimate Media Downloader to be a chill, welcoming space for **everyone**—regardless of your experience level, background, gender, identity, or where you're from. Whether you're a CS freshman who just learned what Git is or a senior dev who's been coding since high school, you belong here.

## TL;DR: Don't Be a Jerk

Seriously, that's the whole thing. But if you need specifics, keep reading.

---

## The Good Stuff (Do This)

- **Be kind and respectful** - We're all here to learn and build cool stuff
- **Be patient with beginners** - Remember when you didn't know what a "pull request" was?
- **Give constructive feedback** - "This could be improved by..." > "This sucks"
- **Accept feedback gracefully** - Even if it stings a little, it helps you grow
- **Use welcoming language** - Keep it inclusive and friendly
- **Help others learn** - Share your knowledge, don't gatekeep
- **Give credit where it's due** - Acknowledge others' contributions
- **Assume good intentions** - Most people aren't trying to be difficult

---

## The Bad Stuff (Don't Do This)

- **No harassment** - This includes unwelcome comments about someone's identity, appearance, or background
- **No trolling or insults** - Save the roasts for your group chat
- **No sexualized language or imagery** - Keep it professional-ish
- **No doxxing** - Don't share others' private info without permission
- **No spam or self-promotion** - I see you, and it's annoying
- **No discrimination** - Period.
- **No unconstructive criticism** - "It's bad" isn't helpful; "Here's how to make it better" is

---

## Where This Applies

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and comments
- Code reviews
- Any project-related communication
- Representing the project publicly (social media, conferences, etc.)

---

## When Things Go Wrong

### If You See Something, Say Something

If someone's being a jerk or making you uncomfortable:

1. **Report it** - Email the maintainer or open a private issue
2. **Block if needed** - Your mental health comes first
3. **Don't engage** - Don't feed the trolls

### What Happens to Violators

Depending on how serious the violation is:

| Level | What Happened | Consequence |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Minor | First-time offense, minor rudeness | Friendly warning |
| Moderate | Repeated issues or more serious behavior | Temporary ban |
| Severe | Harassment, discrimination, doxxing | Permanent ban |

The maintainer has final say on what counts as a violation and what the appropriate response is. I'll be fair, but I won't tolerate toxicity.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
